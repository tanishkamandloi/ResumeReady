How Recruiters Actually Scan a Resume

  • 1. Name & role confirmed instantly
  • 2. Skills scanned, not counted
  • 3. Projects checked for proof
  • 4. Education verified, not admired
  • 5. Red flags = instant rejection

What Makes Recruiters Say YES vs NO

✅ Strong Hiring Signals

  • 1. Evidence over claims - Projects with outcomes, not adjectives
  • 2. Scannable structure - Clear sections, predictable flow
  • 3. Keyword alignment - Skills match job description language
  • 4. Breathing space - Margins & spacing that reduce effort

❌ Instant Rejection Triggers

  • 1. Decorative noise - Photos, icons, progress bars
  • 2. Wall of text - Paragraphs that require effort
  • 3. Self-ratings - “Expert ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐” (cringe)
  • 4. Irrelevant flex - Hobbies, childhood awards, fluff
